Twelleria's World – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Twelleria's World is a single-player game that works completely offline.

We do not collect any data. The game has no accounts, no chat, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no network features.

Everything you create (your name, your look, your pet, your coins, your pottery and holiday photos) is stored only on your own device, in the game's private storage. We cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app.

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photo library.

Because no data leaves your device, we do not share data with anyone, and there is nothing to request, correct or delete from us.

Age: the game is rated 16+ and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, and it contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no external links and no way to talk to other people.

Contact: sidharth.wagle+support@gmail.com

You can read this policy inside the game at any time: tap Privacy on the title screen.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
